How We Work

A process built for real deadlines

No lengthy discovery decks. We move in four stages, each one ending in something you can see, test, or ship.

01

Scope

We spend the first week mapping the problem against your data, systems, and constraints — and tell you honestly if AI is the right tool for it.

02

Prototype

A working version, built against real data, lands in your hands within weeks so you're evaluating something concrete, not a proposal.

03

Build

Our engineers embed with your team to harden the prototype: evaluation suites, guardrails, and the infrastructure to run it at your actual volume.

04

Operate

Post-launch, we stay on to monitor performance and cost, and hand your team full ownership of the system whenever you're ready.
